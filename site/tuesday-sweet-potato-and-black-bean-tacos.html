Tuesday — Sweet Potato and Black Bean Tacos

Suggested photo/search prompt: Tacos filled with roasted sweet potatoes, black beans, and topped with fresh avocado slices.

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

mexicanvegetarianfamily-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 large organic sweet potatoes
  • 2 cans organic black beans (drained and rinsed)
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Peel and cube sweet potatoes. Toss with cumin and salt, then roast for 25 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas in oven during the last 5 minutes.
  4. Mash avocados for topping.
  5. Assemble tacos with sweet potatoes, black beans, and avocado.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure sweet potatoes are well-cooked and mushy for easy eating.
  • Serve filling separately for easy handling.
