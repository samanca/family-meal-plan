Tuesday — Baked Salmon with Sweet Potato Wedges

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

fishsalmonbaked

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 salmon fillets
  • 3 large organic sweet potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Slice sweet potatoes into wedges and toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet.
  3. Bake sweet potatoes for 20 minutes, then add salmon fillets, seasoning them with salt and pepper.
  4. Bake for an additional 15 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Flake the salmon and sweet potatoes well before serving. Ensure no large pieces remain.
