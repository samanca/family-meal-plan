Wednesday — Vegetarian Stuffed Bell Peppers

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetariangluten-free30 minutes

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bell peppers
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 can black beans (15 oz)
  • 1 cup corn
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut tops off bell peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, combine cooked quinoa, black beans, corn, and spices. Fill peppers with the mixture.
  4. Place stuffed peppers in a baking dish and cover with foil. Bake for 25 minutes. Optional: Remove foil, sprinkle cheese on top, and cook for an additional 5 minutes until melted.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut peppers into smaller pieces and scoop out filling to make it easier for the toddler to eat.
