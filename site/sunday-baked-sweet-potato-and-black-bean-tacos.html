Sunday — Baked Sweet Potato & Black Bean Tacos

Suggested photo/search prompt: colorful tacos on a plate

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegangluten-free30 minutes

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 medium sweet potatoes
  • 2 cans black beans (15 oz each)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Wash and pierce sweet potatoes, then bake for 30 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, combine black beans, cumin, and chili powder.
  4. Warm corn tortillas in the oven for 5 minutes.
  5. Once sweet potatoes are soft, scoop out flesh and mix with black bean mixture.
  6. Fill tortillas with sweet potato mixture, top with avocado, lettuce, and tomatoes.

Toddler Notes

  • Mash sweet potato before serving for easier chewing.
  • Serve black bean mixture without skin for a soft texture.
