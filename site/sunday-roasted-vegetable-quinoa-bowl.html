Sunday — Roasted Vegetable Quinoa Bowl

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegangluten-free30 minutes

Ingredients for 6

  • 1.5 cups quinoa
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 red onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Fresh herbs (parsley or basil)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Rinse quinoa under cold water and combine with vegetable broth in the pressure cooker; cook on high pressure for 1 minute, then let it naturally release for 10 minutes.
  3. Chop the vegetables into bite-sized pieces and toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet.
  4. Roast vegetables for 25 minutes, stirring halfway through.
  5. Once quinoa is done, fluff it with a fork, combine with roasted veggies, and garnish with fresh herbs.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure veggies are cut small and soft after roasting to prevent choking hazards.
