Monday — Quinoa Stuffed Bell Peppers

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianquinoabell peppershealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bell peppers (any color)
  • 1.5 cups cooked quinoa
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans (rinsed)
  • 1 cup corn (frozen or fresh)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut the tops off bell peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, mix quinoa, black beans, corn, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stuff the peppers with the mixture and place in a baking dish.
  5. Top with cheese if desired. Bake for 25-30 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve quinoa mix without whole beans for easier eating.
  • Cut bell peppers into manageable pieces.
