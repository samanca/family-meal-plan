Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa and Vegetables

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenquinoahealthyfamily recipe

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 chicken breasts
  • 2 cups quinoa
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 lemon (juiced)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 cups broccoli (chopped)
  • 2 cups carrots (sliced)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Mix olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, and garlic powder. Brush over chicken.
  3. Place chicken on a baking sheet and surround with broccoli and carrots.
  4. Bake for 25-30 minutes until chicken is cooked.
  5. Cook quinoa in vegetable broth as per package instructions.
  6. Serve chicken with quinoa and veggies.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut chicken into small, bite-sized pieces for easy chewing.
  • Ensure vegetables are soft and cut into small pieces.
