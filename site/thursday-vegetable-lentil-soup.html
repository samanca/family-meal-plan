Thursday — Vegetable Lentil Soup

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegansouppressure cooker

Ingredients for 6

  • 1 cup dried lentils, rinsed
  • 3 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in the pressure cooker, sauté onion, garlic, carrots, and celery until soft.
  2. Add lentils, diced tomatoes, thyme, salt, pepper, and broth.
  3. Seal and cook on high pressure for 15 minutes; let release naturally.

Toddler Notes

  • Blend soup slightly to make it smoother for toddlers and ensure no large chunks are present.
