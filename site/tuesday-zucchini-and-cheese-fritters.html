Tuesday — Zucchini & Cheese Fritters

Suggested photo/search prompt: golden zucchini fritters on a plate

Cook Time: 20 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianunder 30 minutes

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 medium zucchinis
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (like cheddar)
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Grate zucchini and place in a clean towel to squeeze out moisture.
  3. In a bowl, mix zucchini, flour, eggs, cheese, and seasonings.
  4. Scoop portions onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes until golden brown.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut fritters into small bites. Ensure soft texture.
