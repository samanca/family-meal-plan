Monday — Pressure Cooker Lentil Soup

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: pressure cooker

soupvegetarianpressure cookerhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cups green lentils
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 carrots (diced)
  • 2 celery stalks (diced)
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper

Instructions

  1. In pressure cooker, sauté onion and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add carrots, celery, and cook for 3-4 minutes.
  3. Add lentils, tomatoes, broth, and spices. Close lid.
  4. Cook on high pressure for 15 minutes. Quick release when done.

Toddler Notes

  • Lentils should be soft; mash if needed for younger toddlers.
  • Cut vegetables into tiny pieces for easy eating.
