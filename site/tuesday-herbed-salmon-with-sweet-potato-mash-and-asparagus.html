Tuesday — Herbed Salmon with Sweet Potato Mash and Asparagus

Suggested photo/search prompt: Herb-crusted salmon with sweet potato and asparagus

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

salmonsweet potatoesasparagusdinner

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 salmon fillets
  • 2 tbsp fresh dill
  • 2 lbs sweet potatoes
  • 1 lb asparagus
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Season salmon with dill, salt, and pepper and place in a baking dish.
  3. Peel and chop sweet potatoes, boil until tender; mash with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  4. On a baking tray, toss asparagus with olive oil, salt, and roast alongside salmon for 15 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut salmon into small flakes, ensure bones are removed.
  • Sweet potato should be smooth and easily mashable.
