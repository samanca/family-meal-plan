Wednesday — Oven-Baked Vegetable Frittata

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianfrittataeggsoven-baked

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic eggs
  • 1 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1 cup spinach (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup onion (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup milk (or almond milk)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a bowl, whisk eggs, milk, salt, and pepper.
  3. Add diced vegetables and stir.
  4. Pour into a greased oven dish and bake for 25 minutes until set.
  5. Cut into wedges to serve.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure frittata is fully cooled before serving to avoid burns.
  • Cut into very small wedges for easy handling.
