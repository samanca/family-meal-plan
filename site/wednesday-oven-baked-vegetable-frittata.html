Wednesday — Oven-Baked Vegetable Frittata

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianegghealthytoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 12 organic eggs
  • 2 cups organic spinach
  • 1 cup organic bell peppers, diced
  • 1 cup organic tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cup organic zucchini, grated
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Whisk eggs in a bowl and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe dish; add veggies and sauté for a few minutes.
  4. Pour eggs over vegetables, stir gently, then transfer to oven.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes until set.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut frittata into small squares for serving.
  • Ensure all vegetable pieces are small and soft.
