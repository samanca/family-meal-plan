Tuesday — Baked Sweet Potatoes with Black Bean Salsa

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: oven

sweet potatovegetarianbakedhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1 can black beans (rinsed)
  • 1 red bell pepper (diced)
  • 1 avocado (diced)
  • 1/4 cup cilantro (chopped)
  • 1 lime (juiced)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Prick sweet potatoes with a fork and bake for 30-35 minutes until tender.
  3. In a bowl, mix black beans, bell pepper, avocado, cilantro, lime juice, cumin, and salt.
  4. Slice baked sweet potatoes open and top with salsa mixture.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure sweet potatoes are soft; mash or cut into small pieces.
  • Use ripe avocado and soft beans for easy chewing.
