Monday — Vegetable and Chickpea Buddha Bowl

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegetarianbuddha bowlchickpeasquick

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cups cooked chickpeas (canned or cooked from dry)
  • 1 cup brown rice
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook brown rice in a pressure cooker according to package instructions.
  2. In a bowl, combine chickpeas, bell pepper, cucumber, carrots, and spinach.
  3. Drizzle with tahini, lemon juice, salt, and pepper; mix well.
  4. Serve in bowls and top with avocado slices.

Toddler Notes

  • Chickpeas should be softened; mash slightly for toddlers.
  • Cut vegetables into small, manageable pieces.
