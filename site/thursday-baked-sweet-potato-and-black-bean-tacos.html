Thursday — Baked Sweet Potato and Black Bean Tacos

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

TacosSweet PotatoBlack Beans

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 small whole wheat tortillas
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, diced
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 avocado, diced

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Toss diced sweet potatoes with olive oil, cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread on a baking sheet and bake for 25 minutes until tender.
  4. In each tortilla, fill with baked sweet potatoes and black beans, and top with diced avocado.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure sweet potatoes are soft and cut into small bits.
  • Tortillas can be cut into quarters for easy handling.
