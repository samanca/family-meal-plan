Monday — Sweet Potato & Black Bean Tacos

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

vegantacossweet potatoquick

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 large organic sweet potatoes
  • 2 cans organic black beans
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 tomato
  • Lime
  • Cilantro (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Peel and cube sweet potatoes, season with salt, and roast for 20-25 minutes.
  3. Warm black beans in a pressure cooker for 5 minutes.
  4. Assemble tacos using tortillas, sweet potatoes, black beans, lettuce, avocado, and tomato.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve sweet potato in small bites to avoid choking.
  • Mash avocado for easier eating.
