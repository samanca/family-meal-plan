Wednesday — Vegetable and Chickpea Curry

Suggested photo/search prompt: A warm bowl of chickpea curry with assorted vegetables served with rice.

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

veganquickhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cans chickpeas (15 oz each)
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, spinach)
  • 1 can coconut milk (13.5 oz)
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 3 cups brown rice

Instructions

  1. Cook brown rice according to package directions.
  2. Combine chickpeas, mixed vegetables, coconut milk, and spices in the pressure cooker.
  3. Cook on high pressure for 10 minutes. Allow to naturally release for 5 minutes.
  4. Serve curry over cooked rice.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure veggies are soft and chopped small.
  • Serve curry with little rice to avoid choking, watery consistency helps.
