Thursday — Quinoa & Veggie-Stuffed Peppers

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: oven

vegetarianquinoastuffed peppershealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bell peppers (any color)
  • 2 cups cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup corn (frozen or fresh)
  • 1 can black beans (rinsed)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut the tops off peppers and remove seeds.
  3. Mix quinoa, corn, black beans, and spices in a bowl.
  4. Stuff peppers with mixture and place in a baking dish. Bake for 30 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure peppers are soft and cut into small pieces.
  • Mash mixture if necessary for easier consumption.
