Tuesday — Baked Sweet Potato & Black Bean Tacos

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegansweet potatooven

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic sweet potatoes
  • 2 cups canned black beans, rinsed
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • Corn tortillas, for serving
  • Avocado, for topping
  • Salsa, for serving

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Wrap sweet potatoes in foil and bake for 30 minutes until tender.
  3. In a bowl, mix black beans, cumin, and chili powder.
  4. Once sweet potatoes are done, cut in half and mash slightly.
  5. Fill corn tortillas with black bean mixture and sweet potatoes, serve with avocado and salsa.

Toddler Notes

  • Make sure to mash sweet potatoes well and cut tacos into small, manageable pieces.
