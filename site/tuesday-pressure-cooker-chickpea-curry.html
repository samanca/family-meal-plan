Tuesday — Pressure Cooker Chickpea Curry

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegancurrychickpeas

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cups canned chickpeas (drained)
  • 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
  • 1 onion (diced)
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • 3 cups brown rice (cooked)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Add onion and garlic to the pressure cooker and sauté for 2-3 minutes.
  2. Add chickpeas, coconut milk, curry powder, cumin, and broth.
  3. Seal and cook on high pressure for 10 minutes, then natural release.
  4. Serve over brown rice.

Toddler Notes

  • Mash chickpeas lightly for better texture.
  • Serve curry on rice, avoiding whole chickpeas.
