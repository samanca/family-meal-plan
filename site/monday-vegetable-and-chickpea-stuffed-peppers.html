Monday — Vegetable and Chickpea Stuffed Peppers

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

VegetarianChickpeasBell Peppers

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 large bell peppers (any color)
  • 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes (canned or fresh)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Slice the tops off the bell peppers and remove seeds. Set aside.
  3. In a bowl, mix chickpeas, brown rice, tomatoes, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stuff each pepper with the chickpea mixture and drizzle tops with olive oil.
  5. Place peppers in a baking dish and cover with foil.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes until peppers are tender.

Toddler Notes

  • Chickpeas should be mashed slightly for tenderness.
  • Cut peppers into small, bite-sized pieces for safe eating.
