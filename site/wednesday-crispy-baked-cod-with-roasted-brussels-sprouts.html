Wednesday — Crispy Baked Cod with Roasted Brussels Sprouts

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

fishhealthyoven

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic cod fillets
  • 2 cups Brussels sprouts, halved
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss Brussels sprouts in olive oil, salt, and pepper; spread on a baking sheet.
  3. In a separate dish, season cod fillets with olive oil, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  4. Bake cod and Brussels sprouts for 20 minutes, or until cod is flaky and sprouts are tender.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut cod into small flakes and ensure Brussels sprouts are soft. Offer smaller pieces to reduce choking hazard.
