Sunday — Baked Herb Chicken & Veggies

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenhealthyoven-bakedtoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic chicken breasts
  • 3 cups organic broccoli florets
  • 3 cups organic carrots, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons dried thyme
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix olive oil, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Coat chicken in the mixture and place on a baking sheet.
  4. Arrange broccoli and carrots around the chicken, drizzle with remaining oil mixture.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes until chicken is cooked through and veggies are tender.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut chicken into small, bite-sized pieces to avoid choking risk.
  • Ensure vegetables are cooked until soft for easier chewing.
