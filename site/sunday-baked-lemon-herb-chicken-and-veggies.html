Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken & Veggies

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenhealthybakedtoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 chicken breasts (skinless)
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 cups baby carrots
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Place chicken breasts on a baking tray and coat with the mixture.
  4. Surround the chicken with broccoli and carrots.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until chicken is cooked (165°F).

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure chicken is shredded for easy chewing.
  • Cut veggies into small, manageable pieces to reduce choking risk.
