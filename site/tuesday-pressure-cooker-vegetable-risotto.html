Tuesday — Pressure Cooker Vegetable Risotto

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegetarianrisottopressure cookerquick

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cups arborio rice
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in pressure cooker and sauté onions until translucent.
  2. Add arborio rice and toast for 2 minutes.
  3. Pour in vegetable broth and add peas and carrots.
  4. Seal lid and cook on high pressure for 10 minutes. Quick release pressure.
  5. Stir in Parmesan cheese and adjust salt to taste.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure risotto is creamy and soft to avoid choking risks.
  • Cut carrots into very small pieces before adding.
