Tuesday — Pressure Cooker Vegetable Risotto

Cook Time: 25 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegetarianrisottoquickfamilypressure cookertoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 1 ½ cups Arborio rice
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • 1 onion (diced)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Set pressure cooker to sauté mode and heat olive oil.
  2. Add onion and garlic, sauté until translucent (3-4 minutes).
  3. Add Arborio rice and toast for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Pour in vegetable broth, carrots, and peas, then close lid and set to manual high pressure for 6 minutes.
  5. Release pressure, stir in Parmesan cheese, and season to taste.

Toddler Notes

  • Allow risotto to cool and portion into small servings for easy eating.
  • Ensure carrots are soft and easily mashable.
