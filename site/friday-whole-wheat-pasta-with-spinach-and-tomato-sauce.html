Friday — Whole Wheat Pasta with Spinach and Tomato Sauce

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

pastavegetarianeasy

Ingredients for 6

  • 1 lb whole wheat pasta
  • 4 cups fresh spinach
  • 2 cups organic tomato sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions. Drain.
  2. In a large bowl, combine cooked pasta, spinach, tomato sauce, and olive oil. Mix well.
  3. Transfer to a baking dish, top with cheese, and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-15 minutes until heated through.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure pasta is cut into small pieces; mix well to avoid clumping.
