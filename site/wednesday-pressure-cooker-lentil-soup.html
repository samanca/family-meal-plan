Wednesday — Pressure Cooker Lentil Soup

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegansouplentils

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cups organic lentils
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 carrots, diced
  • 3 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to the pressure cooker and stir.
  2. Cook on high pressure for 20 minutes. Let pressure release naturally.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve soup in small amounts; blend for a smoother texture if needed.
