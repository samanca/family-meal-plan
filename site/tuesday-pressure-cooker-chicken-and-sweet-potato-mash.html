Tuesday — Pressure Cooker Chicken and Sweet Potato Mash

Suggested photo/search prompt: Creamy sweet potato mash topped with tender shredded chicken.

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

chickenquickpressure cooker

Ingredients for 6

  • 1.5 lbs chicken breasts
  • 2 lbs sweet potatoes
  • 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Peel and chop sweet potatoes, place in pressure cooker with chicken broth.
  2. Add chicken breasts on top, season with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Cook on high pressure for 15 minutes.
  4. Shred chicken and mash sweet potatoes together before serving.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure chicken is shredded into very small pieces.
  • Sweet potato mash can be pureed smoother for easier eating.
