Tuesday — Oven-Roasted Vegetable Medley

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven-roasted

veganoven-roastedeasyhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cups organic carrots, sliced
  • 2 cups organic zucchini, chopped
  • 2 cups organic bell peppers, diced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian herbs
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss vegetables with olive oil, herbs, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet.
  3. Spread in a single layer and roast for 20-25 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut vegetables into small, manageable pieces for toddlers.
  • Cook slightly longer if needed for extra softness.
