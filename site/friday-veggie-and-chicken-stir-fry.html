Friday — Veggie and Chicken Stir-Fry

Suggested photo/search prompt: A colorful veggie and chicken stir-fry over a bed of brown rice.

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

chickenstir-fryhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 chicken breasts (organic, cubed)
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (organic, frozen or fresh)
  • 3 cups brown rice (organic)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce (low sodium)
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil (organic)

Instructions

  1. Cook brown rice according to package instructions.
  2. In pressure cooker, sauté cubed chicken until browned (about 5-7 minutes).
  3. Add mixed vegetables and soy sauce, stir, and seal. Cook on high pressure for 10 minutes. Quick-release pressure.

Toddler Notes

  • Allow to cool and cut chicken into small, manageable pieces.
  • Ensure vegetables are soft and cut small.
