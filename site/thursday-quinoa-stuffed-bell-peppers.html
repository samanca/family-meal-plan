Thursday — Quinoa Stuffed Bell Peppers

Suggested photo/search prompt: Bell peppers stuffed with quinoa and veggies, topped with fresh herbs.

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianquinoastuffed peppers

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bell peppers (any color)
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa (leftover from Sunday)
  • 1 can black beans (organic, drained)
  • 1 cup corn (frozen, organic)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut tops off bell peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, mix quinoa, beans, corn, cumin, salt, and pepper. Stuff into each bell pepper.
  4. Place stuffed peppers in a baking dish. Cover with foil and bake for 25-30 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure stuffing is well-mixed and cool before serving.
  • Cut bell peppers into small pieces.
