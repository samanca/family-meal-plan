Thursday — Baked Sweet Potato & Lentil Mash

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegansweet potatohealthytoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 medium organic sweet potatoes
  • 2 cups cooked organic lentils
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh chives for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Poke sweet potatoes with a fork and bake for 25 minutes until tender.
  3. Slice open, scoop out flesh, and mash with lentils, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  4. Top with fresh chives before serving.

Toddler Notes

  • Mash to a smooth texture for easier eating.
  • Serve in small portions to minimize choking risk.
