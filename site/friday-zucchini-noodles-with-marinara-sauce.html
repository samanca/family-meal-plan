Friday — Zucchini Noodles with Marinara Sauce

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

zucchinivegetarianpasta alternative

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 medium zucchinis
  • 3 cups marinara sauce (no added sugar)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp dried basil
  • 1/4 cup nutritional yeast (optional)

Instructions

  1. Use a spiralizer to create zucchini noodles (zoodles).
  2. In a pressure cooker, heat marinara sauce, olive oil, and basil.
  3. Add zoodles, and cook on high pressure for 1 minute, then quick release.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut zoodles into shorter pieces for easier eating.
  • Check marinara sauce for any chunky ingredients.
