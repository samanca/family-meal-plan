Tuesday — Roasted Sweet Potato and Black Bean Tacos

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

tacosvegetariansweet potatofast

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 large sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 2 cans black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 avocados
  • 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Toss sweet potatoes with cumin, chili powder, salt, and olive oil; spread on a baking sheet and roast for 25 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas in the oven for 5 minutes before serving.
  4. Fill tortillas with sweet potatoes, black beans, sliced avocado, and cilantro; squeeze lime on top.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut avocado slices small; ensure sweet potatoes are tender and easy to chew.
