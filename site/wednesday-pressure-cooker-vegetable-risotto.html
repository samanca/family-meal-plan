Wednesday — Pressure Cooker Vegetable Risotto

Cook Time: 25 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

VegetarianQuickComfort Food

Ingredients for 6

  • 1 1/2 cups Arborio rice
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup peas (frozen)
  • 1 cup carrots (diced)
  • 1/2 cup onion (chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Set pressure cooker to sauté, add olive oil and onion, cook for 2 minutes.
  2. Stir in rice until coated, add broth. Close lid and cook on high pressure for 6 minutes.
  3. Quick release the pressure, add peas, carrots, salt, and pepper. Stir in Parmesan (if using).

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure all veggies are well-cooked and cut into small pieces; serve warm.
