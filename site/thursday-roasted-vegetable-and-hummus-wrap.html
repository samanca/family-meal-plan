Thursday — Roasted Vegetable and Hummus Wrap

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

wrapvegetarianhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 4 large whole grain wraps
  • 1 zucchini (sliced)
  • 1 bell pepper (sliced)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons organic olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup organic hummus

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Toss zucchini, bell pepper, and tomatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper, place on a baking sheet.
  3. Roast for 20 minutes until tender.
  4. Spread hummus on each wrap, top with roasted veggies, and roll up.

Toddler Notes

  • Use smaller pieces of roasted veggies to avoid choking.
  • Cut wraps into small sections for easier handling.
