Friday — Basil Pesto Pasta with Cherry Tomatoes

Suggested photo/search prompt: Pasta tossed with fresh basil pesto and colorful cherry tomatoes.

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianquickfamily-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 1 lb whole grain pasta
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1/2 cup pine nuts
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to package directions.
  2. In a blender, combine basil, pine nuts, olive oil, salt, and pepper. Blend until smooth.
  3. Toss cooked pasta with pesto and halved cherry tomatoes. Serve warm.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut cherry tomatoes to avoid choking.
  • Pasta should be cooked until soft for easier chewing.
