Thursday — Quinoa and Vegetable Stuffed Bell Peppers

Suggested photo/search prompt: Bright bell peppers stuffed with a colorful quinoa and vegetable mixture, ready to serve.

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianstuffedhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bell peppers (any color)
  • 1.5 cups cooked quinoa (from earlier in the week)
  • 1 can organic diced tomatoes
  • 1 zucchini
  • 1 onion
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut tops off the bell peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, mix cooked quinoa, diced tomatoes, chopped zucchini, and onion. Season with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stuff the peppers with the mixture and place in a baking dish. Bake for 25 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Chop filling well and allow it to cool slightly before serving to toddlers.
  • Cut peppers into halves for easy handling.
