Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: oven

chickenquinoabakedhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 chicken thighs
  • 2 cups quinoa
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 lemon (zested and juiced)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons dried thyme
  • 2 teaspoons dried rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon pepper
  • 1 cup broccoli (cut into florets)
  • 1 cup carrots (sliced)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a baking dish, combine chicken thighs, olive oil, lemon juice, thyme, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bake for 30 minutes or until cooked through.
  4. Rinse quinoa and cook with water in pressure cooker for 12 minutes.
  5. Steam broccoli and carrots until tender, about 5-7 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut chicken into small, bite-sized pieces for easy chewing.
  • Serve soft, steamed vegetables; mash if needed.
