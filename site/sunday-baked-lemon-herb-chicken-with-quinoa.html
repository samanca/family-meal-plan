Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa

Suggested photo/search prompt: A plated dish of baked lemon herb chicken with fluffy quinoa and steamed broccoli.

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenquinoaherbsfamily-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 chicken breasts (organic)
  • 1 cup quinoa (organic)
  • 2 cups vegetable broth (organic)
  • 2 lemons (juiced)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (organic)
  • 2 tsp dried herbs (thyme, rosemary)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 pound broccoli (organic)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a baking dish, mix lemon juice, olive oil, herbs, salt, and pepper. Add chicken breast, coating evenly.
  3. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until cooked through.
  4. Cook quinoa in vegetable broth according to package instructions (about 15 minutes).
  5. Steam broccoli until tender (about 5-7 minutes).

Toddler Notes

  • Cut chicken into small, bite-sized pieces to avoid choking risks.
  • Mash quinoa slightly for easier eating.
  • Ensure broccoli is cool and cut into tiny pieces.
