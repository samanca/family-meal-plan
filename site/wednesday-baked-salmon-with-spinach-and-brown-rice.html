Wednesday — Baked Salmon with Spinach and Brown Rice

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven-baked

seafoodhealthyoven-baked

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic salmon fillets
  • 4 cups organic spinach
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Place salmon on a baking sheet; drizzle with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bake for 15-20 minutes until cooked through.
  4. Sauté spinach in a pan until wilted, then serve with salmon and brown rice.

Toddler Notes

  • Flake salmon into small pieces for easier chewing.
  • Serve rice with spinach mixed in for easier eating.
