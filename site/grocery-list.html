Whole Foods Organic Grocery List

Produce

Grains

Meat & Seafood

  • 6 salmon fillets (wild-caught, organic)

Condiments & Oils

  • 2 tbsp olive oil (organic)

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce (low sodium)

Spices & Herbs

  • 2 tsp dried herbs (thyme, rosemary)
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
