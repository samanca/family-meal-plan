Thursday — Vegetable and Lentil Bake

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

veganhealthyvegetarianlentils

Ingredients for 6

  • 1 cup green lentils (cooked)
  • 3 zucchinis (sliced)
  • 3 bell peppers (chopped)
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a baking dish, layer zucchini, bell peppers, lentils, onion, crushed tomatoes, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  3. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Uncover and bake for an additional 10 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure lentils are soft and consider mashing as needed.
  • Cut veggies into small, soft pieces.
