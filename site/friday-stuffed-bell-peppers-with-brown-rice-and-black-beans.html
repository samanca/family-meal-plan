Friday — Stuffed Bell Peppers with Brown Rice and Black Beans

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

veganstuffed peppersquickhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 large organic bell peppers
  • 3 cups cooked brown rice
  • 2 cans organic black beans
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Slice tops off peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, mix brown rice, black beans, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stuff the mixture into each pepper, top with cheese, and place in a baking dish.
  5. Bake for 25 minutes until peppers are tender.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve with the top of the pepper cut off for easy access.
  • Ensure filling is mushy in texture.
