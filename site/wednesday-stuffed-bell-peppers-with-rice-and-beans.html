Wednesday — Stuffed Bell Peppers with Rice and Beans

Suggested photo/search prompt: colorfully stuffed bell peppers

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianin-oven30 minutes

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bell peppers
  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 2 cans black beans (15 oz each)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut tops off peppers, removing seeds.
  3. In a bowl, combine cooked rice, black beans, cumin, and paprika.
  4. Stuff each pepper with the rice mixture and top with cheese.
  5. Place in a baking dish with a little water at the bottom and bake for 30 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Mash filling for easier chewing before serving.
  • Serve half a pepper for a smaller portion.
