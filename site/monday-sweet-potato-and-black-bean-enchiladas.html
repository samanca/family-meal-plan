Monday — Sweet Potato and Black Bean Enchiladas

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetariansweet potatoesblack beansoven-baked

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 large organic sweet potatoes
  • 2 cans (15 oz) black beans (rinsed)
  • 12 organic corn tortillas
  • 2 cups organic enchilada sauce
  • 1 avocado (sliced)
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (optional)
  • Cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Peel and dice sweet potatoes, then steam until tender (about 10 minutes).
  3. Mash sweet potatoes and mix with black beans.
  4. Fill tortillas with sweet potato mixture, roll, and place in a baking dish. Top with enchilada sauce and cheese.
  5. Bake for 20 minutes.
  6. Serve topped with avocado and cilantro.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut enchiladas into small pieces without whole beans.
  • Serve with avocado sides cut thinly for easy eating.
