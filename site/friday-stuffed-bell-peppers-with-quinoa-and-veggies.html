Friday — Stuffed Bell Peppers with Quinoa and Veggies

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianbell peppersquinoaoven-baked

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic bell peppers
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans (rinsed)
  • 1 cup corn (frozen)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut tops off bell peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, mix quinoa, black beans, corn, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stuff peppers with the mixture and place upright in a baking dish.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Uncover and bake for another 10 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut stuffed peppers into small wedges for safety.
  • Ensure filling is cool before serving.
