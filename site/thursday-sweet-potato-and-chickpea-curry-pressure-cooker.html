Thursday — Sweet Potato and Chickpea Curry (Pressure Cooker)

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

curryvegetarianhealthyfamily recipe

Ingredients for 6

  • 4 sweet potatoes (diced)
  • 1 can chickpeas (drained)
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 cup spinach (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add sweet potatoes, chickpeas, coconut milk, broth, and curry powder to the pressure cooker.
  2. Cook on high pressure for 15 minutes, then quick release.
  3. Stir in spinach if using, and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure sweet potatoes are well-cooked and soft.
  • Cut sweet potatoes to ensure easy chewing.
