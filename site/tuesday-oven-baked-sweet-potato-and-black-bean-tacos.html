Tuesday — Oven-Baked Sweet Potato and Black Bean Tacos

Suggested photo/search prompt: Tacos filled with roasted sweet potatoes and black beans, served with avocado slices.

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetariantacossweet potatoes

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 small tortillas (corn or whole grain)
  • 3 large sweet potatoes (cubed)
  • 2 cans black beans (organic, drained)
  • 2 avocados (sliced)
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Toss sweet potato cubes with cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 20-25 minutes.
  3. Warm black beans in a pot until heated through (5 minutes).
  4. Assemble tacos with sweet potatoes, black beans, and avocado slices.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure tortillas are soft and cut into small pieces.
  • Mash sweet potatoes for easier eating.
