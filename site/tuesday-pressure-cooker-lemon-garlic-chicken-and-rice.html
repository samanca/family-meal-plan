Tuesday — Pressure Cooker Lemon Garlic Chicken & Rice

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

chickenpressure cookerhealthytoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic chicken thighs
  • 2 cups organic brown rice
  • 4 cups organic vegetable broth
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon (juiced and zested)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with garlic, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  2. Add chicken to pressure cooker with rice and broth.
  3. Seal the cooker and cook on high for 25 minutes.
  4. Release pressure and fluff rice with a fork; garnish with parsley.

Toddler Notes

  • Shred chicken for toddlers and ensure rice is soft and mushy.
  • Avoid any larger pieces of lemon zest in serving.
