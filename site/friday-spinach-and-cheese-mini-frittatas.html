Friday — Spinach and Cheese Mini Frittatas

Suggested photo/search prompt: Golden-brown mini frittatas nestled in a baking tray, overflowing with spinach and cheese.

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

breakfast-for-dinnerquicktoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 12 organic eggs
  • 3 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or mozzarella)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C).
  2. In a bowl, whisk eggs, add spinach and cheese. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Grease a muffin tin with olive oil, pour in the egg mixture.
  4. Bake for 20-25 minutes until set.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut frittatas into small pieces for easier chewing.
  • Ensure the texture is soft.
