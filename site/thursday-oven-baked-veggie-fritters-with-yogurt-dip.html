Thursday — Oven-Baked Veggie Fritters with Yogurt Dip

Suggested photo/search prompt: Crispy veggie fritters served with a creamy yogurt dip.

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianquicksnack

Ingredients for 6

  • 1 cup grated zucchini
  • 1 cup grated carrot
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs (whole grain)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp dill
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Mix grated zucchini, carrot, breadcrumbs, eggs, salt, and pepper.
  3. Form into patties and place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  4. Bake for 20 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  5. Serve with Greek yogurt mixed with dill.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut fritters into small bites.
  • Ensure the yogurt is plain and smooth for easy swallowing.
