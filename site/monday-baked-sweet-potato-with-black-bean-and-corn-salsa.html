Monday — Baked Sweet Potato with Black Bean and Corn Salsa

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

vegansweet potatoeasyfamilyoventoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1 can black beans (rinsed and drained)
  • 1 cup corn (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 red bell pepper (diced)
  • 1 lime (juiced)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Wash sweet potatoes and poke holes with a fork. Bake for 30-35 minutes until soft.
  3. In a bowl, mix black beans, corn, bell pepper, lime juice, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. Once sweet potatoes are cooked, slice them open and stuff with the salsa mixture.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve sweet potato flesh only, mashed with a fork, to make it easier to eat.
  • Ensure beans are cooked well and mashed if necessary.
