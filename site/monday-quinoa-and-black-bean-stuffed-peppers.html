Monday — Quinoa & Black Bean Stuffed Peppers

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Pressure cooker

vegetarianstuffedpressure cookerquickhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic bell peppers (red, yellow, or green)
  • 1 cup organic quinoa
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 can organic black beans, rinsed
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • ½ cup organic corn
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook quinoa in vegetable broth in pressure cooker for 15 minutes.
  2. While quinoa cooks, slice tops off peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, mix cooked quinoa, black beans, corn, spices, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stuff peppers with the mixture and place upright in the pressure cooker with 1 cup of water.
  5. Seal and cook on high for 10 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve quinoa mixture without pepper for easier feeding.
  • Ensure beans are mashed slightly to prevent choking.
