Monday — Quinoa and Black Bean Stuffed Peppers

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianquinoahealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bell peppers
  • 1 cup quinoa (rinsed)
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 can black beans (15 oz, drained and rinsed)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook quinoa in vegetable broth according to package instructions.
  3. Mix cooked quinoa, black beans, cumin, paprika, and salt.
  4. Cut tops off bell peppers and remove seeds.
  5. Stuff peppers with quinoa mixture and place in a baking dish.
  6. Top with cheese, if desired, and bake for 25-30 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Mash black beans slightly for easier eating.
  • Cut peppers into smaller bites after cooking.
