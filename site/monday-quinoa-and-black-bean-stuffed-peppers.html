Monday — Quinoa & Black Bean Stuffed Peppers

Cook Time: 35 minutes

Method: Oven

vegetarianquinoahealthytoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 large organic bell peppers
  • 2 cups cooked organic quinoa
  • 2 cans (15 oz) organic black beans, rinsed
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 cup organic corn
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup shredded organic cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cut the tops off the peppers and remove seeds.
  3. In a bowl, mix quinoa, black beans, corn, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stuff each pepper with the quinoa mixture and place in a baking dish.
  5. Sprinkle cheese on top if desired.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes until peppers are tender.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve in smaller pieces or mash down for easier consumption.
  • Ensure no skins from peppers are left uncut to avoid choking risk.
