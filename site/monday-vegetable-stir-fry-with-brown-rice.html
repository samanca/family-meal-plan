Monday — Vegetable Stir-Fry with Brown Rice

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegetarianbrown ricepressure cooker

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 cups organic mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell pepper, carrot)
  • 2 cups organic brown rice
  • 4 cups water or vegetable broth
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook brown rice in the pressure cooker, using water or broth as directed.
  2. In a large bowl, toss mixed vegetables with olive oil and soy sauce.
  3. Spread on a baking sheet and roast in the oven at 425°F (220°C) for 20 minutes.
  4. Serve stir-fried vegetables over brown rice, garnished with sesame seeds.

Toddler Notes

  • Chop veggies into small, tender pieces for toddlers and ensure they are fully cooked to a soft texture.
