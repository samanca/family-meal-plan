Monday — Vegetable Stir-fry with Brown Rice

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Pressure Cooker

vegetarianbrown ricestir-fry

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 cups mixed vegetables (bell peppers, carrots, zucchini)
  • 3 cups brown rice (cooked)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce (low sodium)
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp ginger (grated)
  • 1 tsp garlic (minced)

Instructions

  1. Cook brown rice according to package instructions (about 25 minutes).
  2. In a pressure cooker, toss mixed vegetables, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic.
  3. Cook on high pressure for 5 minutes and quick release.
  4. Serve over brown rice.

Toddler Notes

  • Chop vegetables into small pieces for safety.
  • Mash or cut brown rice to a softer texture. Eliminate any crunchy pieces.
