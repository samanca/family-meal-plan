Thursday — Oven-Baked Lemon Herb Chicken Tenders

Suggested photo/search prompt: crispy baked chicken tenders

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

gluten-free30 minutes

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 lbs chicken breast
  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 1 lemon (juiced)
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Cut chicken into strips and marinate with lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper for 10 minutes.
  3. Dredge chicken in almond flour and place on a baking sheet.
  4. Bake for 20-25 minutes until cooked through.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure chicken is cooked thoroughly and cut into small pieces.
  • Use a tenderizer to make chicken softer.
