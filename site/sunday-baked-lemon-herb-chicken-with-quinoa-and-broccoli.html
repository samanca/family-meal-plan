Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa and Broccoli

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenquinoabroccolioven-bakedhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic chicken drumsticks
  • 1 cup quinoa
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 lemons (juiced)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix lemon juice, olive oil, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Coat chicken with marinade and place on a baking sheet.
  4. Bake for 30 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Meanwhile, cook quinoa according to package instructions.
  6. Steam broccoli for about 5 minutes.
  7. Serve chicken with quinoa and broccoli.

Toddler Notes

  • Cut chicken into small, manageable pieces without bones.
  • Ensure broccoli is cut into small florets for easier chewing.
