Thursday — Vegetable and Chickpea Buddha Bowl

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

veganbuddha bowlhealthyfamilyoventoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 2 cups cooked quinoa
  • 1 can chickpeas (rinsed and drained)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • 1 cucumber (diced)
  • 1 avocado (sliced)
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Spread chickpeas on a baking sheet, season with salt and roast for 20 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, combine cooked quinoa, roasted chickpeas, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and sliced avocado.
  4. Drizzle with tahini and lemon juice before serving.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve avocado and veggies sliced into small pieces to prevent choking.
  • Chickpeas should be slightly mashed if needed.
