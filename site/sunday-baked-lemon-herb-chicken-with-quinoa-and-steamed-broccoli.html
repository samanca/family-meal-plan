Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa and Steamed Broccoli

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenquinoahealthyfamilyoventoddler-friendly

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1 cup quinoa
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 lemons (juiced and zested)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix lemon juice, zest, olive oil, garlic powder, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. Rub mixture onto chicken thighs and place them skin-side up on a baking sheet.
  4. Bake for 30 minutes or until chicken reaches 165°F (75°C).
  5. While the chicken is baking, rinse quinoa and boil in water for 15 minutes until fluffy.
  6. Steam broccoli for about 7-10 minutes until tender.

Toddler Notes

  • Serve chicken shredded and skin removed to avoid choking risks.
  • Quinoa should be well-cooked and slightly mashed to reduce texture issues.
