Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa & Steamed Broccoli

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenquinoahealthyquick

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic chicken breasts
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 cups cooked quinoa
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon dried herbs (thyme, rosemary)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Marinate chicken breasts with olive oil, lemon juice, herbs, salt, and pepper for 10 minutes.
  3. Place marinated chicken on a baking sheet and bake for 25-30 minutes.
  4. In the meantime, cook quinoa as per package instructions.
  5. Steam broccoli in a pressure cooker for 3-4 minutes until tender.
  6. Serve chicken sliced, with quinoa and broccoli on the side.

Toddler Notes

  • Slice chicken into small pieces to minimize choking risk.
  • Ensure quinoa is well-cooked for easy chewing.
