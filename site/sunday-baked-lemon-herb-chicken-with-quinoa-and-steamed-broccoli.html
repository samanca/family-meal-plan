Sunday — Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa and Steamed Broccoli

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

ChickenQuinoaHealthyQuick

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 chicken breasts
  • 2 lemons (juiced and zested)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 cup quinoa
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a bowl, combine lemon juice, zest, olive oil, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Marinate chicken for 15 minutes.
  3. Place chicken on a baking sheet and bake for 25 minutes or until cooked through.
  4. Rinse quinoa and cook in vegetable broth according to package instructions (about 15 minutes).
  5. Steam broccoli until tender (about 5-7 minutes).

Toddler Notes

  • Chicken should be shredded for easier eating.
  • Quinoa can be mixed with tiny bits of broccoli.
