Friday — Vegetable and Quinoa Stir Bake

Cook Time: 40 mins

Method: Oven

vegetarianovenquickhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 3 cups cooked quinoa
  • 3 cups organic mixed vegetables (broccoli, carrots, peas)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup shredded organic cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix quinoa, vegetables, olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  3. Transfer to a baking dish, top with cheese if using, and bake for 20-25 minutes.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure veggies are finely chopped.
  • Cool before serving.
