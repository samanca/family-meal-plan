Sunday — Oven-Baked Lemon Herb Chicken with Quinoa & Veggies

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Method: Oven

chickenquinoaovenhealthy

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 organic chicken thighs
  • 1 cup organic quinoa
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a baking dish, combine chicken, olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Add diced veggies around chicken, then bake for 30 minutes.
  4. While chicken is baking, rinse quinoa and cook with vegetable broth according to package.
  5. Serve chicken with quinoa and roasted vegetables.

Toddler Notes

  • Shred chicken for toddlers, ensure all veggies are soft and cut into small pieces to avoid choking.
