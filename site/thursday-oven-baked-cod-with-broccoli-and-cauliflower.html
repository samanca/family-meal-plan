Thursday — Oven-Baked Cod with Broccoli and Cauliflower

Cook Time: 30 minutes

Method: Oven

pescatarian30 minutes

Ingredients for 6

  • 6 cod fillets (about 1.5 lbs)
  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon (for juice)
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Place cod on a baking sheet, drizzle with lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Arrange broccoli and cauliflower around the cod, drizzle with olive oil and season.
  4. Bake for 20–25 minutes until cod is flaky and vegetables are tender.

Toddler Notes

  • Ensure fish is deboned and cut into small pieces for safety.
